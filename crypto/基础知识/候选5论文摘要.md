# A Sea of Coins: The Proliferation of Cryptocurrencies in UniswapV2
- Authors: Manuel Naviglio, Francesco Tarantelli, Fabrizio Lillo
- arXiv: 2502.10512v1 [q-fin.CP] 14 Feb 2025
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.10512v1.pdf
- Dataset: 17,194 tokens launched Oct 10 - Dec 2 2024, collected via Infura/Alchemy, mint/burn/swap events
- Classification: honeypot 15,114 (88%) vs sellable 2,080 (12%) using Honeypot.is + GoPlus
- Key findings:
  - At 10th swap, 94% of NTV trapped in honeypots, 6% sellable; at 1000th swap, 55% vs 45% (Table 1)
  - Total new coins market value: more than half remains locked in honeypots even at 1000th swap
  - Buy-and-hold strategy at 60th swap with 0.016 ETH: 70.9% positive, 29.1% fixed loss -0.018 ETH, max 1056 ETH, but majority profit from honeypots (71.83% positive, 2063% return) illusory
  - Sellable only: 31.98% negative, profit 16.43 ETH on 35.18 ETH investment (46.69%)
  - Only 0.28% of sellable tokens generate profit >1 ETH; excluding outliers, cumulative profit turns negative
  - Without sandwich attacks, strategy would yield negative returns and not exploitable
- Pages: Table1 p10, Figure3 p7, p11-12 sandwich analysis
